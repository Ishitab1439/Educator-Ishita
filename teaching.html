Sustainable Glam, MYP 1 Design

Key concept
Development, with the related concepts markets and trends, and resources.
Global context
Globalization and sustainability.
Statement of inquiry
Development of design solutions is shaped by evolving markets and trends, and responsible use of resources enables sustainable responses in an interconnected global environment.
The task
Students act as eco-conscious cosmetic designers and create a natural, functional lip balm for a target audience, then pitch it. The unit runs for 19 weeks and covers Criteria A to D, and I co-planned it with a colleague.
Assessed
Criteria A to D, with a week-by-week task plan for every strand.

Featured unit plan

Sensing the Environment, MYP 2 Design

Key concept
Systems, with the related concept function.
Global context
Scientific and technical innovation.
Statement of inquiry
Exploring how systems of sensors function within scientific and technical methods to sense and understand the environment.
The task
Students identify a real community problem, research it, and design a sensor-based automated system with an Arduino, which they build and test as a simulation in Wokwi. The unit runs for 19 weeks.
Assessed
Criteria A to D, with scaffolded formative tasks and task-specific rubrics for every band. The unit plan lists Chaitra R and Devendhar (Dev) Bachhu as co-authors.

Igniting Inquiry: A Journey from Basic Questions to Deeper Understanding

What
A case study in a Grade 7 Mathematics classroom at AIMEE International School, Vijayawada.
Approach
Inquiry in five stages: open question, investigation, collaborative discussion, refinement, reflection.
