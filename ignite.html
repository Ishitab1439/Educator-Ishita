The case study

Context
IGNITE is an interschool Hackathon and Makeathon at Sreenidhi International School, where students build ideas into working products.
My role
Operations lead and Operations Mentor. This was a different role from teaching: I took on organising work and learned by doing.
What I did
I guided students to fetch a sponsorship of ₹80,000 for the event. I coordinated the vendors and sponsors, managed logistics, oversaw the operational requirements and troubleshot last-minute challenges. It meant many conversations, decisions and follow-ups.
Result
The event came together with support from sponsors, vendors, collaborators, judges and the wider school community, and ₹80,000 was raised in sponsorship.
