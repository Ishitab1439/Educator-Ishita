What I believe about design

Design begins with a real person and a real problem. We sketch, model and prototype to think out loud, and every mistake is useful data. In the end, I ask every student to make it, test it, fix it again.

  1. 01

    Start with the person

    Before any sketch, students ask who this is for and what it has to do for them. A good brief comes from listening, not guessing.

  2. 02

    Make it to think

    I build my own products with 3D printing, laser cutting, wood and electronics, and I teach students to do the same. A model in your hands teaches what a drawing cannot.

  3. 03

    Mistakes are material

    A print that fails or a joint that does not fit shows exactly what to change. I want students to evaluate honestly and improve, because that is the design cycle.
