DP Design Technology

I am building towards teaching Design Technology in the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (DP). This page shows what I am doing to prepare.

What I am doing now

  1. Aug 2026 to now

    DP Design and Technology professional development. I observe DP Design Technology classes at Sreenidhi International School to deepen my understanding of curriculum implementation, teaching strategies and assessment practices.

  2. Ongoing

    Learning the tools. I work in Autodesk Fusion 360 and in product design, the kind of modelling and making DP students rely on. Read my article.

  3. Jan to May 2025

    3D printing, formally. I completed the three-credit course "3D Printing and Design for Educators" from the National Institute of Technical Teachers' Training and Research (NITTTR) Bhopal, with a score of 82%. See the certificate.

  4. Ongoing

    Making. I build in 3D printing, laser cutting, Internet of Things and electronic systems, wood and simple machines.
