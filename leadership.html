Roles I hold at Sreenidhi International School

  1. Aug 2025 to Jan 2026

    Global Schools Advocate (Cohort 7). I was selected for the United Nations Sustainable Development Solutions Network programme on Education for Sustainable Development. Read more.

  2. 2025

    Mentor, AI Tinkerpreneur 2025. I mentored a high school student in the Atal Innovation Mission bootcamp on artificial intelligence, design thinking and entrepreneurship. Read more.

  3. Oct 2024 to now

    Mentor of Change, Atal Innovation Mission, Government of India. I mentor young innovators as they turn ideas into working solutions.

  4. Teacher mentoring

    Mentor for teachers. I mentor teachers on pastoral care, technology-enabled teaching and the Personal Project. For the Personal Project, I support them in implementing the International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme (MYP) Personal Project, and guide them on project requirements, supervision practice and assessment criteria.

  5. Apr 2026 to now

    ManageBac Implementation and Support Lead. I lead the implementation and effective use of ManageBac across academic programmes and year groups.

  6. Jan 2026 to now

    Grade Lead, Middle Years Programme Year 3 (MYP 3). I lead with a focus on student wellbeing and pastoral care, working with a team of homeroom teachers. Read more.

  7. Dec 2024 to now

    Personal Project Supervisor.

  8. Oct 2024

    Workshop leader, Grassroots Innovation Program (GRIP), ACIC CBIT. I led a hands-on session on digital tools for pitching. Read more.

  9. Oct 2025

    Professional learning session leader. I led a session for colleagues on student voice, choice and ownership. Read more.
