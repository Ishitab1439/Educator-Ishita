Roles I hold at Sreenidhi International School

  1. Oct 2024 to now

    Mentor of Change, Atal Innovation Mission, Government of India. I mentor young innovators as they turn ideas into working solutions.

  2. Teacher mentoring

    Mentor for teachers. I mentor teachers on pastoral care, technology-enabled teaching and the Personal Project. For the Personal Project, I support them in implementing the International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme (MYP) Personal Project, and guide them on project requirements, supervision practice and assessment criteria.

  3. Apr 2026 to now

    ManageBac Implementation and Support Lead. I lead the implementation and effective use of ManageBac across academic programmes and year groups.

  4. Jan 2026 to now

    Grade Lead, Middle Years Programme Year 3 (MYP 3).

  5. Dec 2024 to now

    Personal Project Supervisor.

Moderating at Engage 2025
