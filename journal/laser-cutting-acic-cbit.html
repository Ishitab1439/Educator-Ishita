Hands on with a CO2 laser cutter at ACIC CBIT

[Month Year] Making 2-min read

The situation

Learning never stops when you are an educator. I took part in a one-day training programme on CO2 laser cutting at the Atal Community Innovation Centre (ACIC) at CBIT, hosted by the ACIC CBIT Research and Entrepreneurship Foundation.

What I tried

I got hands-on with the CO2 laser cutting machine.

What happened

The session was not just technical. It gave real insight into how this powerful tool shapes the world around us, from prototyping to real-life applications.

What I learned

I am grateful for the chance to learn, observe and tinker. I am excited to bring this back to my students and enrich our design sessions with more making, more creativity and more purposeful learning.
