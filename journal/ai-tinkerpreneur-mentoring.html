Mentoring a student for AI Tinkerpreneur 2025

[Month Year] Leadership2-min read

The situation

A few days ago, I received an unexpected email. It was not from a student I teach, not from my school, and not even from my city or board. A student reached out to ask if I could mentor her for the AI Tinkerpreneur programme by the Atal Innovation Mission. She had found me through the Mentor of Change network.

What I took on

AI Tinkerpreneur 2025 is a nationwide bootcamp by the Atal Innovation Mission, in collaboration with NITI Aayog and Intel Corporation. It is a nine-week programme that helps high school students across India explore artificial intelligence, design thinking and entrepreneurship, with the aim of building meaningful solutions using AI, guided by mentors from education, industry and research.

For someone who believes deeply in student-led learning, this was not just a kind request. It felt like a responsibility.

What I learned

It was a powerful reminder that educators never stop learning. Mentoring a student I had never taught, remotely, taught me as much about my own practice as it asked of her.
