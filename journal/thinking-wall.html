The Thinking Wall: helping MYP students notice how they think

January 2026 Classroom3-min read

The situation

I wanted students to look at their own thinking, and at how different kinds of thinkers can help one another. The task: read the story of Riya, Aarav, Leena and Omar in "The Four Thinkers and the Forgotten Science Fair" (a critical, a creative, an analytical and a reflective thinker), think about how each character approaches problems differently, then reflect on your own thinking style on a shared board called the Thinking Wall.

What I tried

The board had three stages, plus a space for discussion:

  • Discover your thinking style. Which thinker are you most like, and what type of thinking do you want to practise more?
  • Working together. Why is it powerful when a team has different types of thinkers, and what advice would you give each character before the next fair?
  • Stretch your thinking. Create your own "fifth thinker": what kind of thinker would they be, and how can you use all four types of thinking in your next school challenge?
  • Discussion and action items. What patterns do you notice in how your classmates think, and is there a "right" kind of thinking for every situation?

What happened

Students placed themselves among the four thinkers, explained why a team of different thinkers is powerful, and invented fifth thinkers who combine strengths. In the discussion, one student noticed that classmates think in a very creative way that can sometimes lead to missing the important things, and another that different groups can give each other ideas.

What I am grateful for

Thank you to Ms. Aprajita Ralli for empowering us to be reflective thinkers, and to Gargi Mishra for supporting in this.
