Balanced: a self-management activity for MYP students

[Month Year] Classroom 4-min read

The situation

I wanted my students to explore what it truly means to be balanced, not just in academics but in life. Balanced is an IB Learner Profile attribute, and it connects to the ATL skill of self-management.

What I tried

I designed a simple but meaningful activity to build that skill. Students reflected on the different aspects of their lives, visualised their self-assessments through balance sheets, balance trees and weighing scales, and then asked themselves three questions: Which part of my life feels unbalanced? How does this affect me? What can I do to restore balance?

What happened

The reflection sparked deep conversations and a real awareness of how balance, or the lack of it, affects their well-being. It also encouraged a growth mindset and helped them take ownership of their personal development.

What I learned

Teaching and growing is never a solo journey. The activity was planned with colleagues Natasha Dique and Waseem Aquil, and I am grateful to Aprajita Ralli for guiding us in implementing ATL skills effectively. These moments remind me that education is not just about knowledge. It is about helping students understand themselves.

One question I am still turning over: how do you encourage balance and self-reflection in your classroom?
