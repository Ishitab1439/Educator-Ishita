Draw your vision of the future: line-following robots

[Month Year] Classroom 3-min read

The situation

Designing a robot with school students sounds too advanced. I think students are way ahead of us. Their creativity, knowledge and unique perspectives make them more ready than we imagine.

What I tried

I asked students to draw their vision of the future and show how line-following robots could solve real-world problems. The lesson was designed by my colleague Chaitra R, and I am grateful to her for it.

What happened

The responses blew me away. Students proposed line-following robots for tilling soil, sowing seeds and irrigation in agriculture, line-following pods in ropeways to change transport, and delivery robots designed to move through transit hubs efficiently. One sketch showed a delivery robot following a marked line to take the fastest route from warehouse to house.

What I learned

Learning today is not only about books. It is about exploring several ways of thinking and creating. Teaching is a two-way street, and there is a great deal to learn from the minds we nurture.
