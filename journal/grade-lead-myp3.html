Becoming Grade Lead for MYP 3

Jan 2026 Leadership2-min read

The situation

I was entrusted with the responsibility of Grade Lead for MYP 3 at Sreenidhi International School. This role is not just a leadership opportunity. It is a meaningful learning journey, one that will deepen my understanding of student wellbeing, pastoral care and the everyday experiences that shape learners beyond the classroom.

What I hope to do

I see this as a chance to listen more closely, support more intentionally and contribute to a culture where students feel seen, supported and empowered.

I am especially glad to work alongside a committed and thoughtful team of homeroom teachers. Collaboration with such dedicated educators makes this responsibility both reassuring and inspiring.

What I am grateful for

My sincere thanks to Mr. Malcolm Nicolson and Ms. Aprajita Ralli for placing their trust in me and for seeing potential that I now strive to live up to with integrity and purpose.
