Teaching students to set boundaries

[Month Year] Classroom 2-min read

The situation

Knowing our boundaries matters, and learning to set them should begin in school. I have consistently emphasised this with my students and used a range of strategies to help them understand their limits and protect their well-being.

What I tried

I curated a worksheet inspired by Stephen Covey's principles in The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People. Called "Uncover your boundaries", it is a four-box grid with the prompts: I am allowed to; I feel uncomfortable when someone does this to me; in my perfect world, I won't let people treat me like this; and the new rules I want to make. It reminds students to stay calm, confident and patient when they set a new rule, and to give others time to get used to it.

What happened

It has been rewarding to see how a small practice empowers students to move through life with more confidence and clarity.

What I learned

A small, repeated practice helps students recognise when to say no and set personal and academic boundaries.
