From IGNITE to the SYI Ideathon: a learning management system for teachers

[Month Year] Leadership 2-min read

The situation

A project conceived at IGNITE was presented at the SYI Ideathon in Delhi, where it was selected among the Top 125 out of 2,00,000 entries.

What they built

Four Grade 10 students designed a learning management system to support teachers with planning and time management. It offers a thoughtful alternative to existing market solutions.

Why it matters

Kudos to these Grade 10 students for their innovation and impact.
