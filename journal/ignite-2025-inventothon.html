IGNITE 2025: Inventothon, a celebration of student innovation

5 to 6 Nov 2025 Leadership2-min read

The situation

Sreenidhi IGNITE 2025 Inventothon took place on 5 and 6 November 2025 at Sreenidhi International School. IGNITE is a celebration of student innovation, creativity and design thinking, a platform where students showcase their ideas, challenge assumptions and show the power of inquiry-driven learning.

My part

I was part of the design team behind the event, and the event's host thanked the team for ensuring the best for it.

What I took from it

"Watching students take ownership of their ideas, articulate their thinking, and confidently present solutions reaffirmed the power of agency-driven learning. Proud of our students, proud of our team, and proud to contribute to a community that believes in meaningful learning."

IGNITE 2025 is what led into the next edition. See how I am leading operations for IGNITE 2026.

What I am grateful for

Thank you to the students, the teachers and the whole team, and to Aprajita Ralli and Malcolm Nicolson at Sreenidhi International School.
