MYP Enhancing Curriculum Design Basecamp: a month of learning with Design teachers worldwide

[Month Year] Classroom2-min read

The situation

"Learning is a journey best taken together." I was part of a month-long journey with the MYP Enhancing Curriculum Design Basecamp, hosted by Lenny Dutton.

What I took from it

The Basecamp pushed me to ask better questions, listen to diverse voices and reimagine what Design can look like in our classrooms. It gave me the chance to see the MYP Design framework through new lenses, reimagine the design cycle and, most importantly, connect with inspiring Design teachers around the world.

What I am grateful for

Thank you to Lenny Dutton for her insightful discussions, and to Ms. Aprajita Ralli for her continued support in building an environment where such opportunities are possible. I am carrying these learnings back to my classroom and continuing to grow through collaboration.
