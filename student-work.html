LAMP: a class brainstorm

Students' first ideas for a functional lamp, gathered before any design decisions were made.

MYP Design class, brainstorm
Brief
Design a functional lamp from scratch, following the IB Design Cycle.
Process
An open brainstorm on sticky notes and the whiteboard at the start of the unit.
Learning
The ideas were raw, innovative and insightful, and they reminded me that students bring knowledge of their own.

Line-following robots of the future

Four panels: delivery robots, transport pods, a trash-sensing robot and a robot with cameras.

Student sketch, design lesson
Brief
Draw your vision of the future and show how line-following robots can solve real-world problems.
Process
Students sketched freely in a lesson designed by Chaitra R.
Learning
Ideas ranged from farming robots to ropeway pods and delivery robots in transit hubs.

Butterfly of prints

Rainbow colours printed with cut vegetables inside a drawn butterfly outline.

Grade 7 student, maths through art
Brief
Learn how cutting a solid at different angles changes its cross-section, ahead of the volume of prisms.
Process
Students painted with cut vegetables and printed the shapes they found.
Learning
The shape changed with the angle of the cut, and each picture was different, so each student's perspective showed.
